# Contexts, Words & Macros

Contexts group domain-specific syntax, words define custom operators, and macros use the `@` call prefix. They are part of the language design, but contexts, `use`, word declarations, and word calls are currently blocked by E2010.

Macro calls are accepted with W2009 and have no expansion. Treat this page as a map to the [Contexts](doc:reference-17-contexts), [Words](doc:reference-16-words), and [Macros](doc:reference-15-macros) reference chapters, not as a production tutorial.
