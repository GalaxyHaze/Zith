Raw & Unsafe

raw and unsafe express operations outside ordinary safety guarantees. The specification has stricter rules for raw contexts, pointer access, and the Trust capability.

Use raw code only at a small, audited FFI or platform boundary. The Raw & Unsafe reference is the canonical source; the planned ownership guarantees depend on NRA, which is not yet implemented.
