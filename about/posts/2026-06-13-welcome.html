Welcome to the Zith Diary

2026-06-13

Zith is at v0.2.0. The lexer, parser, semantic analysis, type system and MIR lowering all run end to end, which is far enough along that I have started forgetting why I made half the decisions that got me here. That is what this diary is for.

Most of my time lately has gone into the heuristic engine — the "did you mean?" suggestions — and into the test suite, which is the part nobody writes about and the part that decides whether the rest survives a refactor.

The near goal is unglamorous: compile programs that are actually worth compiling. Self-hosting is the far goal, and I am not going to pretend it is close.

The blog itself is a directory of HTML files on the gh-pages branch. No database, no CMS, nothing to keep running. That is the whole design.

— Haze
