A Detour Through Workflow-AI

2026-06-19

Zith has not moved much this week, and the honest reason is that I have been spending my free time on GalaxyHaze/Workflow-AI instead. It is an AI-powered workflow automation tool, a completely different domain, and building in a different domain turns out to be genuinely restorative.

The cost is real, though. Context-switching between a compiler and an application is not free: the compiler is in a healthy state — the type system is evolving, the resolver is solid, the type-walker infrastructure is in place — but nothing significant has landed while I was away.

What did land is v0.3.1, which captures everything that accumulated before the detour. The critical fixes alone justified cutting it; there was undefined behaviour hiding in the lexer and the source map, and that is not the kind of thing you leave sitting on a branch.

Going forward I am splitting time more evenly. Zith is still the main event.

— Haze
